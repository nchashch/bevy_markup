## Symptoms

Locally, release, all 41 inputs took 8.6–94 s each and up to 1.7 GB; four
hit libFuzzer's out-of-memory at a single 2.2 GB allocation. Shapes:
`{{ [x] * 87408739 }}` (printing) and, in 34 of them,
`{{ [None] * 18446744 ~ '…' }}` (concatenation).

## Root cause

MiniJinja's `[x] * n` makes a lazily repeated sequence of up to 10⁸ items
at no fuel cost, and every operation that walks it deeply is unbounded
(UPSTREAM U62):

- printing with HTML escaping formats the whole value into a `String`
  before escaping it into the bounded output (`MAX_RENDERED_BYTES` never
  sees it), and even a failed write keeps iterating every item
  (`fmt::DebugList` skips formatting but the loop goes on);
- `~`, `in` and `==` on constants are folded at compile time, with no
  fuel: the 34 concatenations stalled while the template *compiled*;
- with variables, `~` builds its string before charging, and nested
  repetitions (`[[n] * 10⁵] * 10⁵`) walk 10¹⁰ items.

## Fix

In bevy_markup (protects crates using the released MiniJinja too): the
environment's formatter refuses a list or map holding more items than the
output could take (three bytes an item, counted through nested ones,
refusing a too-long list by its length without iterating it), and escapes
as it writes. `string`, `tojson`, `pprint` and `json` check the same;
`join` charges fuel for its collection before making it.

In MiniJinja (U62; branch `fix/fuel-for-deep-walks`, patched in here with
`[patch.crates-io]`, ADR 0052): deep walks are charged against the
remaining fuel before they happen, and constant folding declines large
ones. With both, all 41 inputs run in under 100 ms but one, which renders
up to the 16 MiB output limit (26 ms in a release build).
