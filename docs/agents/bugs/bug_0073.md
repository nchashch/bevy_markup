## Symptoms

Enter / gamepad A on a submit button never submitted: only `Click`
bindings fired. And when a form *did* submit, its `Submit` signal's
`source` was always `Pointer { Mouse, Primary, (0,0), 1 }` regardless of
the real pointer, touch or activation.

## Root cause

`activate_element` emitted only the element's click bindings and never
ran the form-submit path; `emit_form_submit` hard-coded the pointer
source.

## Fix

Activation on a submit button submits its form, with the activation as
the `Submit` source (`cd21ffd`).
