## Symptoms and repro
`repeat(1431655766, [a] 1px 1px 1px)`: 3 × 1431655766 = 2³² + 2 wrapped
to 2 in a 32-bit `usize`, slipping under `MAX_GRID_TRACKS`.

## Root cause and fix
Unchecked `usize` multiplication and sum. Fix: `saturating_mul` /
`saturating_add`, and a negative count saturates instead of `as usize`.
