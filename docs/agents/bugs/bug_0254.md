## Symptoms

A 4,102-byte SVG (a 64 px image holding a chain of arcs with radii of
10⁹–10¹⁷) took 6.4 s under ASan and 5 s in a release build, inside usvg's
path conversion, although `svg_bounds` charged it 58k work, far below
the fuzz harness's 524,288 (and `MAX_SVG_WORK`, 2²³).

## Minimal reproduction

```
M1e9 0 A1e9 1e9 1e19 1 1 1e12 1 A1 1e12 1 1 0 1e12 1
```

The second arc (radii 1 and 10¹²) starts, by the exact arithmetic, at its
own end: a zero chord, an empty arc. svgtypes' simplifier makes 4,348
cubics of it; the model counted 285 for the whole path.

## Root cause

`path_segments` predicts each arc's cubics with kurbo's formula
(bug_0190), starting from the exact endpoint of the previous segment.
The simplifier starts from where kurbo's last cubic of the previous arc
ended. For radii up to about 10⁶ the two agree; after an arc of radius
2·10¹⁷ with a huge rotation, the real point was 3.5·10¹⁵ units off. The
next arc then had a chord to cross that the model didn't see, kurbo
scaled its radii up to reach it (to about 10³³), and it became ~105,000
cubics, which svgtypes drains in quadratic time (UPSTREAM U22).

## Fix

`path_segments` tracks `drift`, a bound on the distance between the
simplifier's point and the exact one. It is 0 until an arc whose radius
needs more than `EXACT_SWEEP_SUBDIVISIONS` subdivisions per turn (about
1.5·10⁶) and goes back to 0 at any absolute coordinate (the simplifier
stands on the exact point again); a close restores the subpath start's.
While it isn't 0, an arc is charged a whole turn at the largest radius
its chord, widened by the drift, lets kurbo scale to, and its end may be
that ellipse's diameter further off.

Both fuzz inputs now finish in 1 ms under ASan with CI's limits. Of the
31,833 icons surveyed for ADR 0051, none gets a different work bound.
The test fails without the drift (285 counted against 4,348 built).
