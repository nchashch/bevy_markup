## Symptoms and repro
Two id-less `p`s at the same position in two containers, only the first
after `.hot`: both got `.hot + p`'s background (reversed: neither). With
`[data-x="2"]`, the order of `data-x="1"`/`"2"` decided both.

## Root cause and fix
The key held tag, id, classes, pseudo-state, the ancestors' tag/id/
classes/pseudo and the position — not the dataset or the siblings. Fix
(ADR 0030 §4): `SelectorReads` (computed at load) adds the element's and
ancestors' datasets when an attribute selector exists, the preceding
siblings' prefix hash when a sibling combinator exists, and the ancestors'
positions when a non-subject compound has a structural part.
