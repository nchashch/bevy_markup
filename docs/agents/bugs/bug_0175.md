## Root cause and fix
The unwrap (bug_0172) stopped at `html`'s two children (`head`,
`body`), and the walked-through `body` had no height, which invalidated
the root cursor. Fix: a walked-through element advances its column's
cursor by the sum of its children's heights (`column_height`), and its
children's positions start at its own top. Nodes that produce no item
(ASCII-blank text, comments, `head`/`script`/`style`) advance by zero:
a comment or a `head` used to invalidate the cursor too, and an
NBSP-only text (content) was treated as blank.
